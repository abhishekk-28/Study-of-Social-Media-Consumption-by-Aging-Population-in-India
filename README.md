# Study of Social Media Consumption by Aging Population in India

## Overview

This research project examines social media consumption and usage behaviour among the aging population in India. The study investigates how **attitude, subjective norms, and intention** influence the actual use of social media among older adults.

The research is based on the **Theory of Planned Behaviour (TPB)** and aims to understand the behavioural factors associated with social media adoption among individuals aged 50 years and above.

## Research Objectives

- Examine the attitude of older adults towards social media consumption.
- Analyse social media usage behaviour among the aging population.
- Identify factors influencing social media adoption.
- Examine the intention of older adults towards social media usage.
- Analyse the relationship between attitude, intention, and actual usage behaviour.

## Methodology

The study follows a combination of exploratory and descriptive research approaches. Primary data was collected through a structured questionnaire using a **five-point Likert scale**.

| Research Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Target Population | Social media users aged 50+ |
| Geographic Scope | India |
| Sample Size | 207 respondents |
| Sampling Method | Judgmental Sampling |
| Data Collection | Online & Offline Questionnaire |
| Pilot Study | 12 respondents |
| Research Framework | Theory of Planned Behaviour |

## Data Analysis

The collected data was analysed using:

- **SPSS 20.0**
- **SmartPLS 3.3.3**

The analysis included demographic analysis, factor analysis, reliability and validity assessment, model evaluation, hypothesis testing, mediation analysis, and predictive assessment using R², Q², and F².

## Key Findings

The results indicate that **attitude plays an important role in influencing both intention and actual social media usage behaviour**. Intention also demonstrates a significant relationship with actual usage and mediates the relationship between attitude and behaviour.

In contrast, subjective norms showed limited influence within the proposed research model, including an insignificant mediation effect through intention.

## Practical Implications

The findings provide insights for researchers, policymakers, marketers, and digital platform designers interested in the growing digital engagement of older adults.

The study highlights the potential value of **age-inclusive platform design, digital literacy initiatives, ease of use, and perceived usefulness** in supporting digital participation among the aging population.

## Limitations & Future Research

The study is limited to selected behavioural variables and respondents within India. The use of judgmental sampling may also limit the generalisability of the findings.

Future research could incorporate additional factors such as **perceived ease of use, digital anxiety, and emotional well-being**, while using random or stratified sampling and longitudinal research designs.
